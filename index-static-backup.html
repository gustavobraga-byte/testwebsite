About the Project

The Café MRM project represents a long-term partnership between French and Brazilian research institutions, focusing on specialty coffee, sustainability, and innovation.

Born from a meeting at IGLS 2023 in Garmisch, Germany, between Aziz Galvão (UFV) and Anne Rollet (AMU), this collaboration has grown into a comprehensive research initiative linking managerial innovation with responsible innovation in the context of agroecological transition in Brazil.

Our mission is to transform production systems towards more resilient and biodiversity-respecting models while ensuring transparent traceability that values human work and terroir origin.

Research Themes

🌱

Sustainability

Transformation of production systems towards more resilient models that respect biodiversity. Focus on Atlantic Forest preservation and environmental compliance.

💡

Innovation

Rethinking managerial and frugal processes for small mountain coffee producers. Digital Twin technology for traceability systems.

🤝

Responsibility

Ensuring transparent traceability that values human work and terroir origin. Connecting small producers to premium markets.

📍

Traceability

Digital connectivity and EUDR compliance. Satellite monitoring, blockchain technology, and Participatory Guarantee Systems (PGS).

🌳

Atlantic Forest

Integration of forest preservation into product value. Serra do Brigadeiro State Park - Atlantic Forest biome preservation.

🎓

Digital Twin School

Educational program connecting agronomy and commerce students. Simulation models for supply chain scenarios and market requirements.

EUDR Compliance (European Union Deforestation Regulation)

The new EU regulation requires precise geolocation and proof of non-deforestation. Digital traceability through connectivity becomes non-negotiable for market access.

  • Satellite monitoring + Blockchain certification
  • Proof of origin from farm to port
  • Geolocation at parcel level
  • Integration with Brazilian Forest Code (Código Florestal)
